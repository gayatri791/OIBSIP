# EDA on Retail Sales Data

**Track:** Data Analytics | **Level:** 1 | **Internship:** Oasis Infobyte (AICTE)

## Objective
Explore a retail sales dataset to find sales patterns, customer behaviour and business insights.

## Tools
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Google Colab

## Dataset
"Retail Sales Dataset" (Kaggle, by Mohammad Talib): 1,000 transactions, 9 columns, no missing values.

## What I did
- Inspected the data (shape, data types, nulls)
- Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, standard deviation)
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends (line charts)
- Customer demographics: age groups and gender
- Product category analysis: revenue and units sold
- Correlation heatmap
- Extra analysis: average order value by age group and gender
- Conclusion with 3 business recommendations

## Key findings
- Sales peaked in May and Q4 2023 and were lowest in September.
- Price per unit drives total sales (correlation 0.85) more than quantity (0.37).
- Electronics earned the most revenue; Clothing sold the most units.
- Customers are spread evenly across age groups and genders, with similar spending.
